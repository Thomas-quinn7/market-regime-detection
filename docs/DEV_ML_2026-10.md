## Reading against the paper's claims

Class: development data only (S&P 500, 2005-2023), no holdout, not a claim. Counts over both losses, from the tables above.

- Claim B (the regime probability adds nothing once the VIX is in) against the objection "you only tested a linear nest": with the HMM probability, a flexible learner was 'better' than the same learner without it in 1 of 46 comparisons and 'worse' in 1; the pure-noise regressor was 'better' in 2 of 46. The protocol reading (F1 better while F1n is not) holds in 1 of 46; lightgbm at h = 126 (RMSE) on F0 against HAR + VIX is 'worse'. On development data the objection does not overturn claim B: every protocol finding is in a learner that is itself worse than HAR + VIX at that horizon and loss.

- No learner on F0 beats HAR + VIX: 'better' in 0 of 38 comparisons, 'worse' in 18.

- More lags and the VIX term structure (F2 vs F0): 'better' in 3 of 46, 'worse' in 20; 'better' at h = 5 (table (iii)).

- The race (c): not scored when this file was written; no reading.

- Other regime detectors (d) in place of the HMM probability: 'better' than F0 in 0 of 36 comparisons, 'worse' in 4. The null is not specific to the HMM.

Multiplicity: none of these counts is corrected; at a 2.5% one-sided rate per comparison about 1.2 'better' verdicts in 46 are expected by chance alone, which is what the noise row shows.

## What this would need to become a claim

All of the above is one series (the S&P 500), development years the paper already used to choose its models, and an uncorrected count over many learners, horizons and losses. To become a claim it would need: (1) a protocol frozen and committed before any new data is opened, naming one learner, one feature set, one horizon and one loss as primary, with its family of secondary comparisons and their correction; (2) the January 2027 holdout (Thomas's decision) on data no S8 fit has seen; (3) the run reproduced byte for byte from the frozen script. Until then it is a development note: it answers a referee's objection, it adds no evidence of its own.
